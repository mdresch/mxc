## Procurement approach

Do not assume that hardware, software, licensing, consulting, or external services will be purchased. When a need is identified, document the requirement, alternatives, technical/security constraints, total expected commitment in USD, source evidence, and ongoing cost implications before seeking approval.

## Approval and records

The Project Manager's spending authority is USD 25,000. A procurement or other project commitment above that amount requires Steering Committee approval before commitment. The total project budget is not specified; the USD 25,000 threshold is not a project-wide budget. No other delegated procurement authority is stated.

Retain the need, options considered, evaluation, approval decision, purchase/contract record if applicable, and acceptance evidence. The Charter does not establish vendor-selection criteria, procurement schedule, contract terms, or supplier performance measures; determine these only when a specific need exists and before solicitation or commitment.
