**Source:** [Benefits-Statement.md](Benefits-Statement.md)

**This is the Charter, not the PMP (Project Management Plan).** The PMP is a later *integration* document, built only after every subsidiary management plan below is finalized. Conflating the two — drafting a PMP-shaped document here — was a real, corrected mistake in at least one source project. If you're tempted to add scheduling/cost detail here, stop: that belongs in the Schedule/Cost subsidiary plans, which themselves cannot be drafted yet (see below).

## Subsidiary management plans — real buildability order
Most of the 9 plans can be drafted once the Charter exists. Two cannot be front-loaded:
- **Schedule Management Plan** — needs real duration data from actual work already done. Don't draft against estimates alone.
- **Cost Management Plan** — needs real Resource Allocation + Procurement outputs. Same reason.

Name both explicitly as "not yet buildable" rather than drafting a placeholder that looks finished but isn't grounded.
