## Example Submissions

### Example 1: Sustainability Assessment Process

**Process Name**: Develop Sustainability Assessment  
**Process ID**: 14.1  
**Knowledge Area**: Other  
**Process Group**: Planning  

**Problem Statement**: Modern projects increasingly require environmental impact assessments, but PMBOK doesn't have a dedicated process for sustainability evaluation.

**Proposed Solution**: Create a process that evaluates environmental, social, and governance (ESG) factors, identifies sustainability risks, and develops mitigation strategies.

**Expected Outputs**:
- [ ] Sustainability Assessment Report
- [ ] ESG Risk Register
- [ ] Sustainability Metrics Dashboard

**Dependencies**:
- [ ] Develop Project Charter (4.1)
- [ ] Define Scope (5.3)
- [ ] Plan Risk Management (11.1)

**Business Value**: Enables organizations to meet ESG reporting requirements, reduce environmental impact, and improve corporate social responsibility.

**Implementation Notes**: Should integrate with existing risk management processes and quality standards.

### Example 2: AI Governance Process

**Process Name**: Establish AI Governance Framework  
**Process ID**: 14.2  
**Knowledge Area**: Other  
**Process Group**: Initiating  

**Problem Statement**: AI/ML projects require specialized governance that PMBOK doesn't cover, including model validation, bias testing, and ethical oversight.

**Proposed Solution**: Create a process that establishes AI governance policies, defines model lifecycle controls, and implements ethical AI practices.

**Expected Outputs**:
- [ ] AI Governance Charter
- [ ] Model Validation Plan
- [ ] Bias Testing Protocol
- [ ] Ethical AI Guidelines

**Dependencies**:
- [ ] Develop Project Charter (4.1)

**Business Value**: Ensures AI projects comply with regulations, reduce bias, and maintain ethical standards.

**Implementation Notes**: Should integrate with quality assurance and risk management processes.

### Example 3: Digital Transformation Process

**Process Name**: Execute Digital Transformation Assessment  
**Process ID**: 14.3

- [ ] Change Impact Analysis

**Dependencies**:

**Implementation Notes**: Should coordinate with scope, schedule, and risk management processes.

## Submission Instructions

1. Fill out all sections above
2. Submit to the PMBOK system administrator
3. The system will:
   - Validate process ID uniqueness
   - Check dependency validity
   - Verify artifact type registration
   - Generate quality thresholds
   - Add to process registry
   - Update orchestrator process map
